# Demand scenario configs

Every reported demand figure must trace to a file in this directory.
Nothing is parameterised in scripts.

Calibration common to all scenarios:
  awareness_external 0.10 / awareness_internal 0.40 — calibrated to observed
  stablecoin uptake in markets with ACTIVE SUPPRESSION (Nigeria bank ban
  2021, Argentine capital controls, Lebanese banking collapse). Regional
  on-chain value growth: LatAm +63%/yr, SSA +52%, APAC +69%, global
  stablecoin volume +72% (2025). Treated as a FLOOR: legal distribution
  should exceed it.

  CAVEAT: those are transaction-VOLUME growth rates, not household
  penetration. Volume can rise because existing users transact more. The
  mapping to penetration is loose and unvalidated.
